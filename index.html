Used when a branch office or VoIP subnet loses gateway reachability after a switch port change.

SW-CORE-01# show vlan brief

! 2. Inspect 802.1Q trunk encapsulation & native VLAN mismatch
SW-CORE-01# show interfaces trunk
SW-CORE-01# show interfaces Gi0/1 switchport

! 3. Remediate missing VLAN on trunk link & verify ROAS subinterface
SW-CORE-01(config)# interface GigabitEthernet0/1
SW-CORE-01(config-if)# switchport trunk allowed vlan add 20,30
SW-CORE-01(config-if)# end
SW-CORE-01# show ip interface brief | exclude unassigned
